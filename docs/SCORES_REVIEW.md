## Score basis per build

- **Arms Warrior** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption), adjusted for Forever: Victory Rush, rage from damage no longer cut by armor, separate shorter cooldowns, Spearing Strike. Beta rage bugs make sustain and leveling less certain.
- **Fury Warrior** (med): Classic-era raid reputation (assumption), adjusted for Forever: Bloodthirst lost its heal, Flurry reduced, Death Wish redesigned. Raid demand is speculative until Dec 9.
- **Protection Warrior** (med): Classic-era tank baseline (assumption) plus Forever changes: Vanguard, Bastion, separate cooldowns, rage from damage taken no longer reduced by armor.
- **Holy Paladin** (med): Blizzard calls Holy strongest at single-target healing, with group healing needing planning. Classic-era baseline (assumption) for leveling and farming.
- **Protection Paladin** (med): Forever deliberately turns Protection into a real tank (BLZ-PAL); scores lean on Blizzard's stated intent. Consecration's 4-target cap tempers AoE farming.
- **Retribution Paladin** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption), adjusted for Forever's Holy Strike, seal changes and Twist of Light.
- **Beast Mastery Hunter** (med): Classic-era leveling reputation (assumption) plus Blizzard's note that the pet can tank in solo and small-group content.
- **Marksmanship Hunter** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption), adjusted for Lone Wolf, Sniper Shot and raid-wide Trueshot Aura.
- **Survival Hunter** (low): Low confidence: a new melee design with no Classic equivalent. Based on Blizzard's description of trap and melee builds; petless play via an MM dip into Lone Wolf is an assumption.
- **Assassination Rogue** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption), adjusted for Mutilate, Venom and poison crits.
- **Combat Rogue** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption), adjusted for Hack and Slash and Expose Armor changes.
- **Subtlety Rogue** (med): Classic-era PvP reputation (assumption) plus Forever's Cutthroat and Thousand Cuts.
- **Discipline Priest** (med): Classic-era healer baseline (assumption) plus Forever's Penance and absorb toolkit.
- **Holy Priest** (med): Classic-era healer reputation (assumption) plus Forever's Binding Heal and Prayer of Mending.
- **Shadow Priest** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Forever's Shadowform and Devouring Plague changes.
- **Elemental Shaman** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Forever's Lava Burst and totem changes. The Elemental tree was restructured again on Oct 15 (details unverified).
- **Enhancement Shaman** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Maelstrom Weapon and Blizzard's small-group tanking statement.
- **Restoration Shaman** (med): Classic-era healer reputation (assumption) plus Riptide, Water Shield and raid-wide totems.
- **Arcane Mage** (med): Arcane Blast was redesigned on Oct 15 (stacking spell power), so burst and complexity are uncertain. Classic-era baseline (assumption) otherwise.
- **Fire Mage** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Heating Up and per-mage Ignite.
- **Frost Mage** (med): Classic-era leveling and farming reputation (assumption). Blizzard shortened Cone of Cold specifically to curb kite-farming, so farming is scored a bit lower.
- **Affliction Warlock** (med): Classic-era leveling reputation (assumption) plus Forever's drain talents and Spirit-scaled Life Tap.
- **Demonology Warlock** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Blizzard's note that Demonic Brand suits tanking pets like the Voidwalker.
- **Destruction Warlock** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Incinerate and Bane of Havoc. Petless play via Demonic Sacrifice is assumed viable.
- **Balance Druid** (med): Balance changed a lot in Forever (Eclipse, cheaper Wrath, raid-wide aura); Classic-era baseline (assumption) for the rest.
- **Feral Druid (Cat)** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Shifting Power and form quality-of-life changes; Shifting Power arrived late in the beta.
- **Feral Druid (Bear)** (med): Classic-era baseline (assumption) plus Forever's bear tanking upgrades (Primal Bite, Frenzied Regeneration, Thick Hide).
- **Restoration Druid** (med): Classic-era healer baseline (assumption) plus Wild Growth, Swiftmend and Revive.
